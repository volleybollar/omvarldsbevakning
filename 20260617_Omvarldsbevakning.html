New York City's Department of Education published its AI Playbook in June 2026 following a 45-day public feedback period, establishing clear rules for AI use across all 1,700+ schools. Teachers may use AI for lesson planning, brainstorming and drafting communications, but AI cannot assign grades, make disciplinary decisions or collect biometric data without strict oversight. The playbook includes grade-band-specific guidance and a public registry of all approved AI tools used across the city's schools.
