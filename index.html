Estonia's AI Leap Initiative: 20,000 students receive free Socratic AI tools for guided learning
Estonia has distributed free AI access to nearly 20,000 high-school students as part of its national AI Leap Initiative, partnering with OpenAI and Google to deploy educational versions of ChatGPT and Gemini across 10th and 11th grades. Unlike standard chatbots, Estonia's 'Socratic' AI versions are deliberately built to guide students through reasoning rather than providing direct answers, representing one of the first large-scale national integrations of generative AI into an education system. Stanford University and OpenAI researchers are studying the long-term impact on reasoning, retention, and learning behavior, with early results expected later in 2026.

Finland introduces One Study Right Rule and English-language upper secondary from August 2026
From August 1, 2026, Finland's One Study Right Rule enters into force, automatically terminating a student's earlier study right at the same or lower level when they accept a new degree-leading place — the aim is to open higher education slots to more first-time degree seekers. Separately, general upper secondary education in Finland can now be completed entirely in English from August 2026, while tuition fees are introduced for new students from non-EU/EEA countries. These reforms reflect Finland's ongoing drive to make its education system more equitable and internationally competitive.
