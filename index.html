EU Council calls for human-centred AI in education and empowered teachers
The EU Council approved conclusions on May 11 urging member states to adopt an ethical, safe and human-centred approach to AI in education. Key priorities include strengthening teachers' AI literacy, encouraging purpose-built educational AI tools rather than generic ones, and addressing inequalities in digital access. The conclusions stress that teachers — not AI — must remain at the heart of the learning process and should help design and evaluate AI tools.

Stanford researchers warn that AI used in classrooms without clear pedagogical guidance risks boosting performance metrics without producing genuine, durable learning gains.
