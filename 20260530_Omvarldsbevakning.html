OECD Digital Education Outlook 2026: GenAI supports learning when used with pedagogical intent — but risks metacognitive laziness
The OECD Digital Education Outlook 2026 finds that generative AI can support genuine learning when designed or deployed with clear pedagogical purpose — but that students using general-purpose chatbots may produce better outputs while learning less. Multiple studies show that while AI-assisted students outperform peers on assignments, the advantage disappears or reverses in unsupported exams, signalling risk of 'metacognitive laziness.' The report calls for coherent governance frameworks aligning pedagogical goals, technology procurement, and system-wide oversight.
