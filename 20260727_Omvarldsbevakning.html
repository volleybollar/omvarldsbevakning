OECD Digital Education Outlook 2026: GenAI only improves learning with clear pedagogical purpose
The OECD's flagship 2026 Digital Education Outlook finds that generative AI boosts student performance metrics without producing genuine learning gains — unless integrated with clear pedagogical intent or when teaching is redesigned around it.
