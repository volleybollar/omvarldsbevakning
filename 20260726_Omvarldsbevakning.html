Large-scale study: AI in classrooms only sustains learning gains when pedagogically designed
A study from The Education University of Hong Kong, published July 23, analyzed PISA data from 151,969 students across 19 countries and found that students with AI access produced higher-quality outputs, but that advantage disappeared when AI was removed — unless the tools were designed with intentional pedagogical goals. Researchers concluded that AI's educational value lies in integration with rigorous, student-centered assessment frameworks, not in computational speed alone. The findings closely echo the OECD's 2026 Digital Education Outlook conclusion that technology does not equal transformation.
